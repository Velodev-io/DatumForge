What this pass establishes

  • Actual screenshots retain original publisher links.
  • Visual observations identify crops, overlays and product context.
  • Usage notes distinguish command inputs from model feedback.
  • Raw JSON/CSV preserve the catalog for later implementation.
  • Downloaded candidates that were not visually inspected remain labeled.

What remains open

  • Every conditional dialog across all releases and add-ins.
  • Exact font, pixel dimensions, default docking and DPI behavior.
  • Full Revolve, Sweep, Shell, Draft, Pattern and Mirror panels.
  • Explode, Mate Controller, full drawing-sheet and BOM setup.
  • Complete Simulation, CAM, Electrical, Routing and Visualize flows.
  • Hover/focus, drag/drop, errors, cancel recovery and timing.
